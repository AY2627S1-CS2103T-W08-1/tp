# MediConnect

[![CI Status](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-W08-1/tp/actions/workflows/gradle.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-W08-1/tp/actions)

![Ui](images/Ui.png)

**MediConnect is a desktop patient-care coordination application for chronically ill patients and their
caregivers.** It helps family caregivers in Singapore keep each patient's care information in one place instead of
across messaging threads, paper notes, and memory.

MediConnect is designed to:

* maintain patient profiles and care information;
* organize medications, allergens, care instructions, contacts, and appointments;
* show daily medication times and consolidated patient details; and
* support local backup, restoration, and export for care handovers.

Its keyboard-first command interface makes records quick to update and retrieve, while its graphical interface keeps
them easy to review. MediConnect supports care coordination but does not provide clinical advice, contact healthcare
providers, or book appointments.
